Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together on your Brooklyn elopement or intimate wedding.

What is your photography style?

I describe my work as classic, intimate, documentary-style with an editorial edge. I blend candid photojournalism with composed environmental portraits for a timeless, emotional result. My aesthetic is candid and editorial, elevated but never stiff. I focus on authentic moments rather than posed shots or forced smiles.

Do you shoot film?

Yes! Standard packages offer digital only, but you can add 35mm film for that extra lush, nostalgic texture. I spent years in the darkroom and have a deep appreciation for film grain and the timeless quality it brings to images.

What areas do you cover?

I'm based in Brooklyn, NYC and specialize in Brooklyn courthouse elopements and intimate weddings throughout New York City. I'm also available to travel for destination elopements.

How far in advance should I book?

I recommend booking 2-3 months in advance, especially for peak season (May-October). However, I understand elopements can be spontaneous, so reach out even if your date is soon and I'll do my best to accommodate you.

What is your pricing structure?

Pricing varies based on coverage duration, turnaround time, and deliverables. My fee reflects both shooting time and extensive post-processing including careful selection, editing, and gallery curation. Investments can start at as little as $750 for an engagement shoot. Reach out for a detailed quote tailored to your specific needs.

How many photos will we receive?

You'll receive anywhere between 30-60 images per hour of coverage, usually more. The exact number depends on the pace of your day, locations, and how many people are involved.

What types of weddings do you photograph?

I specialize in Brooklyn courthouse elopements, intimate weddings, backyard ceremonies, rooftop celebrations, and non-traditional weddings. If you're keeping it small and prioritizing authentic moments over big production, we're a great fit.

What is your turnaround time?

You'll receive a few social media previews within 24-48 hours. Complete galleries are delivered within 2-4 weeks depending on your selected package and time of year.

Are you LGBTQ+ friendly?

Absolutely. I photograph all people regardless of ethnicity, age, race, class, gender identity, disability, body shape or size, culture, or religion. Love is love, and I'm honored to document yours.

Do you require a contract and deposit?

Yes, a signed contract and 50% deposit are required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due one week before your elopement or wedding date.

What should we wear for our elopement?

Wear whatever makes you feel like yourselves. Whether that's a traditional wedding dress, a vintage suit, jeans and a nice top, or anything in between. The best photos happen when you're comfortable and confident in what you're wearing.
